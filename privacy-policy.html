Privacy Policy

Application: Pancake Café Sort · Last updated: October 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how QuatroIT ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the mobile application Pancake Café Sort (the "App"). By using the App you agree to the practices described below. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

1. Who we are

The App is developed and published by QuatroIT. For any questions about this Policy or about your data, contact us at: pancake.quatro@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account or to provide your name, email, or other personal contact details to play. The App collects the following categories of data automatically or through third-party services:

  • Advertising data. We show ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, AdMob may process your device's advertising identifier, IP address, general device information (model, operating system, language), and interaction with ads.
  • Game progress and account data. We use Unity Gaming Services (UGS) to save your game progress (such as your current level, in-game syrup, boosters, daily login streak, and tutorial state) to the cloud so it can sync across your devices. For this we create an anonymous player identifier. If you choose to sign in with Google Play Games, we also store the identifier provided by that service to link your saved progress.
  • Leaderboard data. The App shows a public leaderboard ranked by the highest level reached. It displays your level together with a player name. The player name is generated automatically unless you choose your own; please do not use your real name or other personal information as a player name.
  • Purchase data. In-app purchases are processed by Google Play. We receive confirmation of the purchase (the product and a transaction identifier) to deliver the item to you and to restore purchases. We never receive or store your payment card or billing details.
  • Push notification token. We use Firebase Cloud Messaging to deliver notifications and to keep your account active on a single device at a time. This uses a device messaging token. Reminder notifications (for example, about your daily streak) are scheduled on your device and only shown if you allow notifications.
  • Analytics data. We use Google Analytics for Firebase and Unity Analytics to understand aggregate usage of the App (such as sessions, levels played, boosters used, and purchases made) so we can balance and improve the game. This data is collected in a pseudonymous form.
  • Local data on your device. Some settings (sound, music, vibration, language) and progress are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to us except as part of the cloud save described above.

We do not knowingly collect your real name, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location (GPS), or payment card information.

3. How we use the data

  • To operate the game and save your progress;
  • To sync your progress across devices and keep your account secure;
  • To deliver in-app purchases and restore them;
  • To show the leaderboard;
  • To show advertising, which keeps the App free;
  • To send notifications you have allowed;
  • To analyze usage in aggregate and improve the App;
  • To detect, prevent, and address technical issues, fraud, or abuse.

4. Legal basis and your consent (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, we ask for your consent before showing personalized ads and before collecting analytics, using a consent form provided through Google's User Messaging Platform. You can change your choice at any time in the App under Settings → Ads & data. Where we rely on consent, you may withdraw it at any time. We also process certain data on the basis of our legitimate interest in operating, securing, and improving the App, and on the basis of contract when delivering in-app purchases.

5. Third-party services

The App relies on the following third-party services, which process data under their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob, Google Play Billing, Google Play Games, and Google APIs — https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services — https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Firebase and Google Analytics for Firebase — https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Unity Gaming Services and Unity Analytics — https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

6. Advertising choices

You can opt out of personalized advertising at the device level. On Android, open Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads) and delete or reset your advertising ID. Within the App, EEA/UK/Switzerland users can also adjust their consent choice at any time. Purchasing "No Ads" removes interstitial ads; optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch remain available.

7. Children

The App is intended for a general audience aged 13 and older. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with data, please contact us and we will delete it.

8. Data retention and deletion

We keep your cloud-saved progress for as long as your account remains active. You can delete your account and associated cloud data at any time, after which it is permanently removed from our systems. To delete, use Delete account in the App (Settings, at the bottom of the list), contact us at pancake.quatro@gmail.com, or see our Account & Data Deletion page for full instructions. Purchase records kept by Google Play are governed by Google's policies. Locally stored data is removed when you uninstall the App.

9. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your personal data, to object to processing, and to data portability. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at pancake.quatro@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

10. Data security

We use the security measures provided by our service providers (Google and Unity), including encryption in transit, to protect your data. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. International data transfers

Our service providers may process and store data on servers located outside your country of residence, including in the United States. Where required, such transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards under the providers' privacy frameworks.

12. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this Policy periodically.
