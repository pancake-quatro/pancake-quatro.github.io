Account & Data Deletion

Application: Pancake Café Sort · Developer: QuatroIT · Last updated: October 7, 2026

This page explains how to delete your Pancake Café Sort account and the data associated with it. Pancake Café Sort does not ask for your name, email, or other personal contact details to play; your progress is tied to an anonymous player identifier (and, if you chose to sign in, your Google Play Games identifier).

Option 1 — Delete from within the App (recommended)

If you have the App installed, this is the fastest and most complete way:

  1. Open Pancake Café Sort.
  2. Tap the gear icon in the top-left corner to open Settings.
  3. Scroll to the bottom of the list and tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm in the dialog.

This permanently deletes your account and cloud-saved data from our systems and removes any link to Google Play Games. The deletion applies to every device signed in to that account. After deletion the game continues as a new guest with empty progress.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you no longer have the App installed, or cannot use the in-app option, contact us:

  • Email: {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}
  • Subject: Pancake Café Sort — Account Deletion Request
  • If you signed in with Google Play Games, please send the request from the email address linked to that Google account so we can locate and verify the data. Otherwise include your Player ID, shown in the App under Settings → Player ID.

We will action your request and confirm by email within 30 days.

What data is deleted

  • Your anonymous player account and any linked Google Play Games identifier;
  • Cloud-saved game progress: current level, in-game syrup, boosters, daily login streak, and tutorial state;
  • Your leaderboard entry and player name;
  • Session and push-notification token data stored with your account.

Data is removed permanently and cannot be recovered. Purchases already made are not refunded by deleting the account; records of purchases kept by Google Play are governed by Google's policies. Anonymized or aggregated analytics that no longer identify you may be retained. Data stored only locally on your device is removed when you uninstall the App.
